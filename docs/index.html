The Binding

Tether your ESP32 by USB. Bid the raven enter, and the right gate shall open of its own accord.

Choose the vessel kindred to your hand

If the binding falters, hold the BOOT rune, tap RESET, release BOOT — then bid the raven enter. The C5 vessel must oft be coaxed by hand into the download trance. Binding now fully erases the flash first, so a boot-looping vessel is reforged clean — no vessel is ever truly bricked.

Seeing only runic garble in the Serial Monitor? That is the on-chip bootloader crying out at 115200 baud — the raven's own firmware has not woken. Set the monitor to 115200 to read its lament, then re-bind (BOOT+RESET) to reforge.

Web Serial is only spoken by Chromium-born browsers (Chrome, Edge, Opera) on desktop.
