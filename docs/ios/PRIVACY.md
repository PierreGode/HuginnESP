# HuginnESP — Privacy Policy

Effective: 27 November 2026

HuginnESP is the iPhone and Android companion app for the Huginn, an open-hardware ESP32 Wi-Fi and Bluetooth survey device. This policy explains what the app does with data. In short: **we don't collect any data.** Everything the app records stays on your phone unless you choose to share or upload it.

## What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account of its own, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash-reporting service. The developer has no server that receives data from the app.

## What the app stores on your phone

- **Drives.** While you record a drive, the app saves what your Huginn reports (Wi-Fi networks, Bluetooth devices and any alerts) together with your position. Drives are stored only on your phone. You can delete any drive from the Drives tab, and deleting the app removes them all.
- **Upload accounts (optional).** If you enter an account for WiGLE, WDGWars or Wardrift under Settings → Upload accounts, those details are kept on your phone so the app can upload drives on your behalf.

## Location

The app uses your location only while you are recording a drive, to place what the Huginn finds on the map. Because drives are usually recorded with the phone locked on a dashboard or in a pocket, location updates continue in the background for the length of the drive, and the phone shows that recording is on: a Live Activity on iPhone, a notification ("HuginnESP is recording a drive") on Android. The app never asks for location "all the time". Location use stops when you stop the drive. Your location is never sent to the developer.

## Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth only to connect to your Huginn. It does not connect to, control or interfere with any other Bluetooth device. When the Huginn flags a nearby device, such as an item tracker separated from its owner or a suspected card skimmer, that notice is shown only to you.

## When data leaves your phone

Only when you choose to:

- **Sharing a CSV.** When you share a drive, the file goes wherever you send it through your phone's share menu.
- **Uploading to a mapping service.** If you set up an upload account and upload a drive, the app sends that drive directly to the service you chose (WiGLE, WDGWars or Wardrift). What happens to it there is covered by that service's own privacy policy. The app cannot create or delete accounts on those services.

The demo Huginn produces made-up data, and demo drives are never uploaded anywhere.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.
